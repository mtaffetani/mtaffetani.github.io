      <h3>Morphing in Slender Structures</h3>
      <p>We explore the hidden mechanics that make slender and thin-walled structures bend, buckle, wrinkle, and snap. From arches and curved strips to shells, we are moved by the question <i>what determines how and when a structure changes shape, forms patterns, or jumps to a new stable state?<\i> Using mathematical modeling, stability analysis, and numerical simulations, I uncover how geometry, curvature, internal stresses, and even tiny imperfections govern these dramatic behaviors. The works in this project section reveal not just when structures fail, but how they <b> organize into ridges, wrinkles or bistable configurations <\b>, providing principles that guide the design of responsive, bio-inspired, and functional materials capable of harnessing instabilities and patterns for novel mechanical functionality.</p>
